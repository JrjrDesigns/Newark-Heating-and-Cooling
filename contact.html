Call us

Phone: (740) 527-0222

Calls are answered 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, for no-heat and no-cooling emergencies. Routine repairs, tune-ups and replacement estimates are scheduled during regular business hours, Monday through Saturday.

When you call, it helps to know roughly how old your system is, what it is doing (or not doing), and whether any error light is blinking on the furnace. If your furnace is not running, our no-heat checklist covers the safe checks to try first.

Request a call back

Prefer not to call right now? Use the form at the bottom of this page with your name and phone number, and we will call you back, usually within the hour during business hours. For an emergency, please call instead of using the form.

From the field

The most useful thing you can tell us on the phone is the blink code from the little window on the furnace door. It often tells us which part failed before we even get there, which means we arrive with the right part on the truck.
