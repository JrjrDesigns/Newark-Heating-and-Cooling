From the field

The question we get most is "what does a new furnace cost?" The honest answer is that the furnace is only part of it. Venting, gas line, ductwork and the permit are where two quotes for the "same" furnace can end up two thousand dollars apart. We list everything that is included so you can compare fairly.

What pushes a price up or down

  • Access. A furnace in an open basement is quicker to replace than one in a crawl space, attic or tight closet.
  • Venting. Switching from a chimney to PVC venting, or relining a chimney for an 80% furnace, adds time and material.
  • Ductwork. Undersized returns and leaky ducts are common, and fixing them during a replacement is money well spent.
  • Electrical. Some heat pumps and high-efficiency systems need a new circuit or disconnect.
  • Timing. After-hours emergency visits cost more than scheduled weekday service.
  • Equipment tier. Two-stage and variable-speed equipment costs more and delivers quieter, more even comfort.
From the field

In rural parts of the county on propane, the furnace price is only half the math. Propane costs more per unit of heat than natural gas, so the efficiency of the new furnace, or whether a heat pump makes sense, has a bigger effect on what you pay every winter.

Questions we hear

HVAC cost questions

How much does a new furnace cost in Licking County?

Most gas furnace replacements run about $2,800 to $5,500 installed, depending on efficiency, size and venting. Propane furnaces run a little higher.

How much does it cost to replace a furnace and AC together?

A combined replacement typically runs about $7,000 to $13,000, and doing both at once usually costs less in labor than two separate jobs.

What does a service call cost?

A standard diagnostic visit runs about $75 to $125. After-hours emergency visits cost more, and we tell you that fee before we dispatch.

Are HVAC prices higher in winter?

The equipment price does not change much by season, but scheduling is tighter during cold snaps. If your system is still running, the fall before the first hard freeze is the easiest time to replace it.

Do you offer financing?

Financing is available on replacements through equipment manufacturers and lending partners, subject to approval. Ask when we give you your written estimate.
