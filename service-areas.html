Other communities we serve

We also serve the smaller towns and townships between them, including Alexandria, Etna, Hanover, Kirkersville, St. Louisville, Jacksontown and Summit Station. If you are in Licking County and are not sure whether we cover your address, call (740) 527-0222 and ask. The answer is almost always yes.

From the field

Licking County is big enough that the heating challenges change as you drive across it. Newark and Granville have some of the oldest housing in the county. Pataskala and the western townships have the newest. The north and south ends have more propane and all-electric homes than town does. We plan every visit around what we expect to find in that part of the county.

Response times

Same-day service is typical across the county on weekdays. During the first hard cold snap of the season, when every furnace in the county is asked to work at once, we prioritize homes with no heat at all and households with seniors, infants or medical needs. We will always give you an honest arrival window on the phone.

From the field

The drive times that matter most are on the coldest nights. Knowing the back roads between Newark, Utica and Johnstown, or the quickest route down to Buckeye Lake when Route 79 is slow, is part of getting heat back on before a house gets dangerously cold.

Questions we hear

Service area questions

Do you serve all of Licking County?

Yes. We serve every community in Licking County, including the rural townships. Call (740) 527-0222 to confirm your address.

Do you charge extra for rural areas?

Standard service calls cost the same across our service area. After-hours emergency visits carry an emergency fee, which we tell you on the phone before dispatching.

Do you serve outside Licking County?

Our focus is Licking County, including the Licking County side of New Albany and Buckeye Lake. Call if you are just over the county line and we will tell you whether we can help.

Where are you based?

We are based in Newark, which puts most of the county within a reasonable drive.

How fast can you get to me?

Same-day service is typical. No-heat calls are answered 24/7, and we give you an honest arrival window when you call.
